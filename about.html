Bayan means clarity. It's my name and my job.

بيان

In Arabic, bayan is a statement made plain. I bring that to software: I find the work in a business that repeats, and replace it with a system that is clear to use and runs on its own.

I'm a software engineer from Gaza. I work mostly on the backend and on AI tooling: Python and LangGraph for agents and pipelines, MCP and integrations to connect them to real tools, and Laravel when a product needs a full web app around it.

I work with business owners, startups and small teams in the Gulf, the US and Europe. Most projects run two to three months with a clear scope from the first week.

What clients can expect

    You always know what I am building, why, and when it lands. Weekly updates in your language, no jargon.

  • Attention to detail

    Edge cases, wrong inputs and the boring parts get the same care as the demo. That is where systems usually break.

  • Fast, with care

    I move quickly because the scope is clear, not because corners are cut.
